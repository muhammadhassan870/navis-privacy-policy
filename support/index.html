Frequently asked questions

How do interviews work?
Open the Interviews tab, choose a category and an interview, then tap “Take Interview”. A 3D interviewer asks questions and you answer out loud. Your report appears in the History tab when the interview ends.

What permissions does NAVIS need?
Microphone and Speech Recognition to record and transcribe your answers, and Camera for optional gaze/engagement feedback during the interview. You can change these in iOS Settings at any time.

How do credits work?
Each interview uses credits. Credit packs are one-time in-app purchases through Apple, and purchased credits do not expire. Use “Restore Purchases” on the Buy Credits screen if a purchase did not appear.

Refunds
Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Profile › Account › Delete Account. You can also email us to request deletion.
