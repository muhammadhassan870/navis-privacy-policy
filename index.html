NAVIS Privacy Policy

Neurodiverse Technologies, Inc. · Effective October 1, 2026

1. Who We Are and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Neurodiverse Technologies, Inc. (“Neurodiverse Technologies,” “NTs,” “NAVIS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects information when you use the NAVIS mobile application, websites, interview-practice services, administrative portals, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

NAVIS is an interview-readiness and practice platform. It may use artificial intelligence and automated analysis to conduct practice interviews, adapt follow-up questions, analyze responses, and provide practice-oriented feedback and readiness information.

2. Our Neurodivergence and Disability Privacy Principles

NAVIS is intended to support a wide range of users, including autistic, neurodivergent, disabled, anxious, and other job seekers. We apply the following principles to disability- and neurodivergence-related information:

  • You do not need to disclose an autism diagnosis, disability, medical condition, or accommodation need merely to use ordinary NAVIS interview-practice features unless a particular optional service clearly requires that information.
  • NAVIS is not a diagnostic tool and is not intended to diagnose autism, anxiety, ADHD, disability, mental-health conditions, personality, honesty, intelligence, or medical conditions.
  • We do not intend to sell disability, neurodivergence, health, eye-gaze, heart-rate, or interview-response information.
  • We do not intend to use disability or health information for targeted advertising.
  • NAVIS practice scores and feedback are not intended to determine whether a person is employable or qualified for employment.
  • We do not intend to penalize a user merely for neurodivergent communication characteristics, including reduced eye contact, atypical prosody, different response timing, or other differences that may reflect disability or neurotype rather than job capability.
  • Optional sensor or health-related features should not be required to access the core interview-practice experience unless we clearly disclose otherwise.

3. Information We May Collect

A. Account and Contact Information

Depending on how you access NAVIS, we may collect information such as your name, email address, telephone number, login or authentication information, contact preferences, organization or business affiliation, and account settings.

B. Professional and Career Information

We may collect job titles, target roles, job descriptions, career interests, work-related preferences, interview selections, employment-related information you choose to provide, and information needed to personalize interview practice.

C. Interview Content and Performance Information

When you participate in a NAVIS interview, we may process your spoken or typed responses, transcripts, answer content, question history, adaptive follow-up paths, scores, practice metrics, feedback, progress, and other information generated from your interaction with the interview experience.

D. Audio, Video, Camera, Microphone, and Eye-Gaze Information

If you enable features that use your device camera or microphone, NAVIS may process audio, video, facial-region or gaze-related signals, speaking pace, pauses, response timing, or similar signals for interview practice and feedback. The production implementation must disclose whether raw recordings are stored, whether analysis occurs on-device or on servers, and the applicable retention period. NAVIS does not represent eye-gaze information as a medical diagnosis or as a definitive measure of employability.

E. Apple Watch, Health, and Sensor Information

If NAVIS offers an optional Apple Watch, HealthKit, heart-rate, or sensor-based feature, we may request permission to access only the data types necessary for the feature. Such information will be treated as sensitive. We will not use HealthKit or health-related information for advertising or unrelated marketing, and we will not sell such information. The specific data types, purpose, storage, retention, and sharing must be disclosed when the feature is activated.

F. Device, Usage, Diagnostic, and Technical Information

We may collect device and application information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the Services, such as device type, operating-system version, app version, identifiers, IP address, timestamps, crash data, performance data, security logs, feature interactions, and usage events, depending on the production analytics and infrastructure used.

G. Payment and Transaction Information

If you purchase credits, subscriptions, or other access, payments may be processed by Apple or another authorized payment provider. We may receive transaction status, purchase identifiers, entitlement information, billing-related records, and other information needed to administer your purchase. We do not need to receive full payment-card numbers when a payment processor handles them directly.

H. Information You Voluntarily Disclose

Interview answers may contain information you choose to disclose about disability, neurodivergence, health, education, employment, family circumstances, or other personal matters. Please avoid sharing information that is not necessary for interview practice. If sensitive information is voluntarily provided, we will process it only as reasonably necessary to provide and protect the Services, comply with law, or as otherwise described to you.

I. Information from Organizations and Other Sources

If you access NAVIS through a school, university, vocational rehabilitation provider, nonprofit, employer, workforce program, or other organization, that organization may provide account, eligibility, assignment, role, or administrative information about you. The organization’s own privacy practices may also apply to information it independently controls.

4. How We Use Information

  • Create and administer accounts and authenticate users.
  • Deliver interview practice, adaptive questioning, scoring, feedback, progress tracking, and recommended resources.
  • Personalize interview scenarios and job-specific practice.
  • Operate optional camera, microphone, eye-gaze, wearable, or sensor features when enabled.
  • Process purchases, credits, subscriptions, entitlements, refunds, and re-attempts.
  • Provide customer support and respond to user requests.
  • Send transactional, security, administrative, and, where permitted, marketing communications.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, security incidents, technical failures, and violations of our Terms.
  • Measure and improve product performance, accessibility, reliability, and user experience.
  • Create aggregated or de-identified information for analytics, product improvement, research, and service planning where permitted.
  • Comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise, or defend legal rights.

5. Artificial Intelligence and Automated Analysis

NAVIS uses artificial intelligence and automated analysis as part of the interview-practice experience. AI may help select or generate questions, interpret response content, identify response characteristics, produce practice scores, create feedback, or recommend areas for improvement.

  • AI outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or context-dependent and should be treated as practice guidance rather than objective facts about a person.
  • NAVIS is not intended to make autonomous hiring, firing, promotion, compensation, or other employment decisions on behalf of an employer.
  • If personal information is sent to a third-party AI or processing provider, the production application and this policy must accurately identify the relevant categories of data and purpose of the processing.
  • NTs should contractually restrict service providers from using NAVIS user data for their own unrelated purposes and, where available and appropriate, disable provider training on NAVIS customer content.
  • Where consent or another legal basis is required for AI processing, NAVIS will provide the required notice and control before the processing occurs.

6. Sensitive Information and Inferences

NAVIS may process information that can be sensitive depending on the feature and jurisdiction, including disability-related statements, health-related information, audio/video, gaze-related signals, and employment information. We do not intend to infer or label a user’s autism status, medical diagnosis, mental-health condition, race, religion, sexual orientation, political beliefs, or other sensitive trait from interview behavior unless a future feature is separately designed, legally reviewed, transparently disclosed, and appropriately consented to.

7. How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information to the following categories of recipients when reasonably necessary:

  • Service providers that host, secure, maintain, analyze, communicate, or otherwise support NAVIS.
  • AI, speech, transcription, analytics, crash-reporting, email/SMS, authentication, payment, and cloud infrastructure providers actually used in production.
  • Organizations through which you receive NAVIS access, but only according to the applicable account configuration, agreement, notice, and permissions.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, investors, acquirers, or transaction participants subject to appropriate confidentiality or legal safeguards.
  • Government authorities, courts, regulators, or other parties when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not intend to sell personal information for monetary consideration. If our practices ever constitute a “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted-advertising disclosure under an applicable privacy law, we will provide the notices and choices required by that law.

8. Schools, Universities, Workforce Programs, Employers, and Other Organizations

NAVIS may be offered through institutional accounts. Before an organization can access identifiable candidate information, NAVIS should define the permitted data fields and access level. Depending on the program, an organization may be able to see account status, assigned interviews, completion, progress, scores, or other information disclosed at enrollment or in the program notice. Raw recordings, transcripts, sensitive sensor data, disability information, and health information should not be made available to an organization by default unless the feature, agreement, user notice, and applicable law specifically support that access.

Where an organization acts as the controller, business, educational institution, employer, or other legally responsible party for certain data, its privacy notice and agreement with NTs may govern that processing in addition to this Policy.

9. Children and Minors

NAVIS is not directed to children under 13 unless NTs has intentionally established a compliant program with appropriate parental, school, or other legally valid authorization. If NAVIS is offered to users under 18, NTs will apply age-appropriate notices and safeguards and will address applicable parental-consent, educational-record, contractual-capacity, and state-law requirements before launch of that use case. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete or otherwise address it.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing the Services, maintaining user progress, meeting contractual commitments, resolving disputes, protecting security, and complying with legal obligations. Different categories may have different retention periods.

Before production publication, NTs must maintain an internal retention schedule specifying, at minimum, retention for accounts, interview transcripts, raw audio/video if stored, derived interview metrics, eye-gaze data, optional health/sensor data, support records, transaction records, and security logs. When information is no longer needed, we will delete, de-identify, or otherwise dispose of it in accordance with our retention practices and applicable law.

11. Your Choices and Controls

  • Camera, microphone, notifications, Bluetooth, HealthKit, and other device permissions can generally be managed through your device settings, subject to feature functionality.
  • Optional sensor or wearable features may be declined or disabled unless clearly identified as necessary for a particular optional service.
  • Marketing emails may be unsubscribed from using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in the message. Transactional and security messages may still be sent.
  • Where available, account settings may allow you to review or update certain account information.
  • If you have a NAVIS account, the app will provide a method to initiate account deletion where required by Apple or applicable law.

12. Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of certain personal information; to opt out of certain processing; to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and to appeal certain privacy-request decisions. These rights may be subject to exceptions and verification requirements.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section below. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Authorized agents may submit requests where permitted by law.

13. Account Deletion

If NAVIS allows account creation, users may initiate account deletion through the application where required. Deleting an account may remove or de-identify associated personal information, subject to information we are permitted or required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes. Deleting the app from a device does not itself delete an NAVIS account.

14. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information appropriate to the nature of the data and risks involved. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting their credentials and notifying us of suspected unauthorized account access.

15. International and Interstate Processing

NAVIS and its service providers may process information in the United States and other locations where providers operate. When legally required, we will use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers. The production policy should be expanded before actively targeting users in jurisdictions with additional requirements, including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, or Canada.

16. De-Identified and Aggregated Information

We may create aggregated or de-identified information that is not reasonably intended to identify an individual and use it for product improvement, research, accessibility analysis, service planning, benchmarking, or other lawful purposes. Where required by law, we will maintain de-identified information in de-identified form and will not attempt to re-identify it except as legally permitted to test or validate de-identification methods.

17. Research and Product Improvement

NAVIS may use appropriately protected information to evaluate and improve interview content, accessibility, AI quality, scoring reliability, and user experience. Identifiable or sensitive information should not be used for unrelated research or model training without an appropriate legal basis, contractual safeguards, and additional notice or consent where required. Research conducted with universities or other partners may be subject to separate consent, institutional review, or study documentation.

18. Third-Party Services and Links

The Services may interact with Apple, payment services, authentication providers, cloud services, AI providers, or external websites. Their independent privacy practices apply to information they collect directly from you. We encourage you to review their privacy notices.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as NAVIS evolves or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required. If a material change affects how we use sensitive information, we will provide any notice or consent required by applicable law before applying the change.
